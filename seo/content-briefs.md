# Content briefs — techyverve.in

Four articles that fill the gaps in `keyword-clusters.md`. The site has no blog yet, so create a `/blog` section
first (a `/blog/[slug]` route + `BlogPosting` schema + a link in the footer and navbar).

**What these briefs are not:** a SERP analysis. Competing pages were not reviewed, so the "competitive notes" are
deliberately left as work for the writer. Before writing, search the primary keyword and note what the top five
pages cover, their length and their format. Also check every legal or tax claim (GST, PF, ESI, TDS) against the
government source on the day of writing; rules and thresholds change.

**Rules for all four:** only claim what TechyVerve really did; use real screenshots from the case studies; give a
clear next step (contact form) rather than keyword-stuffing; one author byline with a founder's name.
